Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Abhi Doubt Solve.ai website handles information when you use this frontend. Please read it before using the service.

1. Information you provide

When you create an account or sign in, information you submit—such as your email address and profile details—is processed to provide account and website features. Account authentication is provided through Supabase. Please do not submit information that is not needed to use the study-help service.

2. Questions and image attachments

When you submit a question or attach an image, the website sends that content to its /api/chat backend to generate an answer. The backend uses Google Gemini as its AI provider. Do not submit passwords, financial details, private documents, or other sensitive information.

3. Storage and retention

Account information may be processed or stored by Supabase to provide authentication and related account features. Questions and images may be processed by the website backend and Google Gemini. The exact retention period and whether server logs or chat records are retained depend on the deployed service configuration and the providers’ applicable policies. The website owner should confirm these settings before publishing this policy.

4. How information is used

Information is used to provide account features, respond to study questions, maintain and secure the service, and troubleshoot technical issues where applicable.

5. Service providers

The website uses Vercel for hosting, Supabase for authentication, and Google Gemini to generate AI responses. Information necessary to provide these services may be processed by these providers under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the website and sign out of your account. Signing out does not necessarily delete information already processed or stored by the website or its service providers. For account or data deletion requests, contact the website owner using the contact details below.

7. Security

The website uses a server-side backend to process AI requests. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share sensitive or confidential information through the website.

8. Children

This website is a general study-help service. A parent or guardian should supervise a child’s use and ensure that the child does not submit personal or sensitive information

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s features or data practices change. The updated date at the top will be revised when changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: examcharge@gmail.com.
